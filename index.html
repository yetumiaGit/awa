About AWA Electrical

AWA Electrical, Data and Maintenance Services PTY LTD is a professional electrical and technical services company based in Cape Town.

We specialize in industrial, commercial, and hazardous environment installations, delivering solutions that meet strict safety and regulatory standards.

Our team combines technical expertise, compliance knowledge, and practical experience to ensure every project is completed efficiently, safely, and to the highest industry standards.
